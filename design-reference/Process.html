How It Works

From first call to grand opening.

A clear, mutual process. We get to know each other, you get the full picture, and we move forward only when it's right for both sides.

01

Request information

Tell us about yourself and your target market. We'll send the franchise discovery packet and set up an intro call.

02

Discovery call

A relaxed conversation about the model, your goals, capital, and timeline — and a chance for all your questions.

03

Review the FDD

We share the Franchise Disclosure Document. Take the time you need to review the details with your advisors.

04

Discovery Day

Visit a NeuMed clinic in Houston. Meet the team, see operations firsthand, and experience the brand in person.

05

Mutual decision & signing

If we're a fit on both sides, we award the territory and sign the franchise agreement.

06

Site selection & buildout

We help you secure real estate and build out the clinic to NeuMed's spa-inspired design standard.

07

Training & hiring

Your team trains on clinical protocols, the wellness menu, and operations. We help you staff and prepare to launch.

08

Grand opening

Launch with a local marketing campaign and ongoing support to ramp toward maturity.

Typical timeline from signing to opening: 9–14 months, depending on real estate and permitting. Illustrative only.
